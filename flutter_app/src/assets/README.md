# PROTO artwork

The initial catalog uses original, code-drawn SVG product illustrations in
`src/components/ProductArtwork.tsx`. All eight illustrations were created for
this PROTO prototype using local `react-native-svg` primitives. They do not use
third-party product images, downloaded assets, or external image services.

The cream, orange, and sage packaging and PROTO lettering are fictional visual
placeholders, not representations of stocked products. Replace them with
approved product photography and verified packaging when the real catalog is
available. Parent product cards supply accessible product names; the decorative
SVG is hidden from accessibility focus.

The app makes no remote requests to render this artwork.
